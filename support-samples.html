    Sample conversation. Personal details are fictional.

    The situation
    Fitly subscription, inbound call. The caller sees a charge they don't recognise and wants it reversed. They're frustrated and talking fast.
    Goal
    Calm the call, find the charge, fix it or explain it
    Tone
    Calm, warm, steady pace
    After the call
    Notes on the account and a recap email
    What I learned at the bank
    • Let the caller finish. Most people calm down once they feel heard.
    • Verify identity every time, even when the caller is in a hurry.
    • Never promise what I can't control. Give real timelines.
    • Leave notes good enough that the next agent doesn't have to ask again.
    0:00

    Greet and own it

    Friendly, clear, and I take ownership from the start.

    Thanks for calling Fitly, this is Raf. I'm sorry you're dealing with this, let's sort it out together.
    0:20

    Listen, then verify

    I let them explain everything first, then confirm their identity before looking at the account.

    Got it, thank you for walking me through that. Before I open your account, can I confirm your email and the last four digits of the card?
    1:30

    Find the cause

    The charge is an annual renewal after the free trial. I explain it plainly, without blaming them.

    I see it now. This is the yearly plan that started after the trial ended on March 2. I understand that's not what you expected.
    2:40

    Offer the fix

    Within policy, I offer a full refund and switch them to monthly, or cancel, and let them choose.

    Since it renewed only 3 days ago, I can refund the full amount today. Would you like to keep a monthly plan or cancel completely?
    3:50

    Recap and set expectations

    Exactly what happens next and when, so they don't have to call back.

    The refund is done on our end. Your bank usually shows it in 5 to 7 business days, and I'm sending you a confirmation email now.
    4:30

    Close and document

    Check if there's anything else, thank them, then write clear account notes and tag the ticket.

    Is there anything else I can help with today? Thanks for your patience, have a good rest of your day.
    How I work a ticket

    The same steps, every channel

    Whether it's a chat, a call or an email, I follow a simple routine so nothing gets missed.

    Read it fully

    Check the history and past tickets first, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.

    Acknowledge

    Show I understand the problem and how it affects them, in one or two honest lines.

    Verify

    Follow the security steps before touching the account. Every time.

    Resolve

    Fix it within policy, or escalate to the right team with all the details already filled in.

    Set expectations

    Clear next steps and real timelines, never vague promises.

    Document

    Tag the ticket and leave clean notes so the next person can pick it up in seconds.
