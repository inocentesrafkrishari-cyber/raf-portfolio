A welcome sequence that turns a free pass into a booked class

Five short emails that go out after someone claims a free class. Each one has one job. The sequence stops on its own the moment they book, so nobody gets emails that don't apply to them.

✓ Stops when the contact books a class

How it runs in GoHighLevel

The workflow behind the emails. Tags keep the CRM clean and show the studio where every lead is.

Trigger: Free class form submitted→ Tag: free-pass→ Email 1→ Wait 1 day→ Email 2→ Wait 2 days→ Email 3→ Wait 2 days→ Email 4→ Wait 2 days→ Email 5→ Booked? Stop and tag: trial-booked
